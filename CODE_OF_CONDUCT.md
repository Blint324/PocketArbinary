That means people of all ages are welcome to this project, from 0-150, you're truly free.  
Your race, gender, sexuality, all of those are equally welcome. All that matters are your contributions to this project.  

## There are, however, ground rules that every contributor, in any shape or form must follow:
- No bullying, harrasment, or discrimination of any kind. That means spreading hateful messages, discrimatory ideologies, and non-tolerant & misinforming biased information.
- Zero generative A.I, of any kind is accepted in this project. That means ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, etc. None of those are accepted in the main branch or in pull requests, and it **MUST** be clarified if you are using it.
- No intentionally malicious code of any kind is allowed. That means intentionally crashing Arduino's, spreading viruses over serial connections, none of those are welcome here.
- Requesting to be a moderator (Writer, maintainer, admin, etc.) in any form, will be ignored. Moderators are picked manually by admins through a human review process, there are no moderator requests. This does **not** however mean that you need permission to commit. Any person can create a pull request, however to have higher permissions will be approved and selected by an existing admin.
- Any sort of sexual material, advances, or requests will be met with an immediate de-modding and loss of respect. That means that if you are caught doing any of those afformentioned things, very limited or none of your pull requests will be approved, **Permanently**. As stated, this community is for **ALL** ages.
## Scope
These rules apply to every branch of this project, including temporary branches, fixes, any violation of these rules on **any** sort of this project will be met by consequences.
